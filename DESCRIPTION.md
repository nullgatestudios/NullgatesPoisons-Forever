**Nullgate's Poisons** is a rogue addon for **World of Warcraft: Forever**. Two buttons apply the poison you chose to each weapon at the highest rank you carry, and the addon warns you before a poison wears off or runs out of charges.

No more opening your bags before every pull, applying the wrong rank, or noticing halfway through a boss that your off hand has been clean for ten minutes.

> **Beta:** this is an early test version and we're looking for rogues to try it. If something doesn't work, run `/npoison check`, then send us the results with a short description.

---

## Features

- **Main Hand and Off Hand buttons**: each applies your chosen poison at the highest rank in your bags. Each button shows the poison's icon, the time and charges left on that weapon, and how many applications you're carrying.
- **Every poison type**: Instant, Deadly, Crippling, Mind-numbing and Wound, plus Forever's Numbing, Atrophic, Sebacious and Occult poisons. Right-click a button to switch.
- **Expiry warnings**: a reminder when a weapon has no poison, less than 5 minutes left or fewer than 10 charges. You choose both limits, and whether the warning is a chat line, a sound or a glow on the button.
- **Bag counts** for every poison type, and optionally how many more you could craft from the ingredients you carry.
- **Key bindings** for both buttons, under Key Bindings > AddOns.
- **Simple settings**: lock and move the bar, vertical or horizontal layout, scale, warning limits and the language.

---

## How It Works

1. **Pick a poison for each hand.**
2. **Click Main Hand or Off Hand**, or press its key binding.
3. **The highest rank you carry is applied** to that weapon.

The game doesn't allow buttons to change during combat. If you switch poisons or your bags change mid-fight, the buttons show a small red mark and update as soon as combat ends.

## Slash Commands

| Command | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| `/npoison` or `/poisons` | Open the settings. |
| `/npoison lock` / `/npoison unlock` | Lock or unlock the bar. |
| `/npoison reset` | Reset the bar's position. |
| `/npoison check` | Test what the addon needs in your game client and save the results (for bug reports). |

---

## Installation

1. Download the latest file.
2. Extract the `NullgatesPoisons-Forever` folder into the `Interface\AddOns` folder of your World of Warcraft: Forever installation.
3. Log in on a rogue. The bar appears near the bottom of the screen; type `/npoison` for the settings.

---

## Good to Know

- The addon only runs for rogues.
- It never buys, sells or crafts anything for you.
- Available in 10 languages: English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Português (Brasil), Русский, 한국어, 简体中文 and 繁體中文 (every language the game client runs in). The game's language is used automatically, and you can pick another in the settings. The translations were made with machine assistance; corrections are welcome.
